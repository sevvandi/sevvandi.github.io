## Overview

Graphons are limits of graph sequences, capturing a graph's structure in a form that doesn't depend on its size. This mini-course is a practical introduction to graphons, sparse graph extensions, graphon estimation, and their use in modern machine learning, including GNN transferability and neural network pruning. It combines short videos, slides and interactive Jupyter worksheets.

**Prerequisites:** basic graph theory, linear algebra and calculus.
